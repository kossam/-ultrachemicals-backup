Target Products & Services

PVA paints

We manufacture and distribute PVA paints which plays a key role in creating the smooth finish, adhesion and durability of the products. Our PVA paints can be used in Interior walls and Ceilings, Masonry and Concrete, high sheen washable water based paints or Decorative for decorative finishes for introduction projects.

Gloss Paints

Our gloss paints offers a smooth, high-gloss surface due to their shiny, reflective finish. They can be used for both residential and commercial purposes for surfaces that require a durable and aesthetically pleasing finish. We manufacture and distribute Oil-Based Gloss Paints, Water-Based Gloss Paints and Alkyd-Based Gloss Paints which can be applied for painting wood trim, doors, furniture , outdoor metal surfaces such as fences, garage doors to help againts rust and corrosion and for industrial use to protect machinery and steel structures from rust and wear and also for marine industry to protect boats and ships against seawater and aesthetic appeal.

Adhesives

We manufacture and distribute adhesive substances for bonding or attaching materials together. They range from liquids to solids for industries including construction, automotive, packaging and healthcare. These includes Rubber-based adhesives, Pressure-sensitive adhesives (PSA), Super glue(cyanoacrylate), Hot glue, Epoxy etc.

Preservative Paints

Formulated coatings designed to protect surfaces from environmental degradation and damage enhancing their durability and extending their lifespan. Our preservative paints are not only limited to aesthetic finish but also serve as barrier againts moisture, UV radiation, corrosion and other harmful factors. We do supply Wood Preservative, Metal preservative, Concrete preservative, Masonry preservative and Marine coatings

Cleaning Solutions

We provide a wide range of cleaning solutions for various applications, including industrial, commercial, and household use. Our products are designed to effectively remove dirt, grease, stains, and other contaminants while being safe for the surfaces they are applied to. These include Degreasers, Disinfectants, Floor Cleaners, Glass Cleaners, Dishwashing Liquid, Pine Gel and Multi-purpose Cleaners.

Sealants

We manufacture and distribute sealants products that can be used to fill gaps, joints or cracks in various structures and surfaces so as to prevent the passage of fluids, gases, or contaminants. Our sealants helps to create airtight, waterproof and weatherproof seals. We supply Bitumen expansion joint sealant, Polyurethane Sealants, Acrylic sealants and Butyle Sealants.

Automotive Paints

We Manufacture and distribute coatings for finishing and protecting the surfaces of vehicles such as cars, trucks, motorcycles and other transportation equipment. Our paints are formulated to provide a durable, aesthetic finish while also offering protection against harsh environmental factors which vehicles are exposed to, such as UV rays, moisture, road salts and physical wear. These types of Paints include Acrylic paints, Polyurethane Paints, Epoxy Paints, Basecoat/Clearcoat System, Meatallic Paints and Matte Paints. Whether you are restoring a classic car or adding a fresh coat to a newer vehicle, dont hesitate to get in touch.
